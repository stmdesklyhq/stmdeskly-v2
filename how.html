HOW IT WORKS

What a Desk is

A Desk is a single page — your page. Not a profile, not a feed, not a chat. Just one clean page with everything about you: your name, your work, your photos, your hours, and how people can reach you.

Every Desk is free. Every Desk looks like a real website. Not a template, not a linktree — a page. If it looks hand-coded, that's because we built it to feel that way.

Who it's for

Anyone who needs to be found.

  • A person — a photographer, a tailor, a phone repairer, a designer. Show your work, put the link in your bio.
  • A business — a shop, a food brand, a fashion label. Show what you offer, where you are, how to order.
  • A place — a church, a mosque, a school, a venue. Share your service times, your programmes, your location.

You don't need to know how to build a website. You don't need a laptop. You need a phone and five minutes.

How to make one

Three steps. No tutorials. No setup.

  • Create. Sign up. Pick a link. Add your name, what you do, one photo. Your Desk exists from that moment.
  • Make it yours. Choose the look. Pick your type, your colours, your layout. Add your services, your photos, your location, your hours. Come back and change it any time.
  • Share the link. Put it in your bio. Send it in a chat. Anyone who opens it sees your Desk — clean, fast, on any phone.

What people see

When someone opens your Desk, they see exactly what you filled in. Nothing more, nothing less.

If you didn't add a section, it just doesn't appear. Nothing looks empty. Nothing looks broken. Your Desk shows what matters and stays quiet about the rest.

At the bottom of every section, there's one way for them to reach you: WhatsApp. One tap and they're in your messages. That's how conversations start.

The Assistant — if you want one

Every Desk has an optional Assistant — a small floating button people can tap to ask questions.

It's not AI. It's not a chatbot. It's a trained FAQ. You teach it what to say: your hours, your prices, your location, how to order. It only says what you taught it — it never invents, never lies, never pretends to be you.

When it doesn't know something, it hands the conversation to you on WhatsApp. And you can see every question anyone has asked it — so you can learn from them.

It's off by default. You choose whether to turn it on.

Free — for real

STMDeskly is free. Not a free trial, not a limited version, not "free until you want more." Just free.

No card. No premium tier. No ads. No selling your data. Your Desk stays up for as long as you want it to.

Every other platform charges monthly. A tailor in Yaba, a church in Ibadan, a photographer in Lekki — they shouldn't need a subscription to be found. That's the whole reason STMDeskly exists.

What a Desk is not

  • Not a website builder — no drag-and-drop, no pages within pages. One page.
  • Not a social network — no feed, no followers, no likes.
  • Not a marketplace — no payments, no cart. Orders happen in conversation.
  • Not a replacement for Instagram or WhatsApp. It sits beside them.

Instagram is discovery. WhatsApp is conversation. Your Desk is where someone lands when they want to know who you are.

Ready?

Making a Desk takes about a minute. No card. No code. Just your place on the internet.
