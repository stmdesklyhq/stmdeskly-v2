THE PLAIN VERSION

Terms & Privacy

Written so anyone can understand it. Not lawyer language.

1. What STMDeskly is

STMDeskly gives you a free, single page on the internet — your Desk. It shows who you are, what you offer, and how people can reach you. You don't need to code. You don't pay. You get one link, and it looks like a real website.

STMDeskly is made by STMGraph, a small team based in Nigeria.

2. Your account

You need an email address to create a Desk. That's it. You can use Google to sign up, or use an email and password.

Your password is stored securely — we never see it. Keep it safe. If you forget it, you can reset it from the login page.

You're responsible for what happens on your account. Don't share your login.

3. What you can post

Anything that's yours and honest. Photos of your work, your services, your hours, your location. All fine.

What's not allowed:

  • Scams, fraud, or fake businesses
  • Anything that harms or threatens another person
  • Content you don't own — stolen photos, fake logos
  • Adult content, hate speech, illegal goods

If we find this kind of content, we'll remove the Desk and, depending on the case, the account. No warnings for the serious stuff.

4. What's public, what's private

Your Desk is public. Anyone with the link can open it — name, photos, services, WhatsApp, everything you filled in. That's the point.

Your account is private. Email address, password, session — only you can see these.

We don't sell your data. We don't run ads. We don't track you across other websites.

5. Free — for real

STMDeskly is free. Not a trial, not a limited tier. You don't need a card. There's no premium version hidden behind a paywall.

If we ever add paid features, they'll be optional and clearly marked. Everything you use today stays free.

6. Service and limits

We try to keep STMDeskly running every day, but we can't promise it never breaks. If the site is down, your Desk will be back up when we fix it.

We may add or remove features. We may change how the Desk looks. We won't delete your account without telling you first, unless you've broken these terms.

7. Liability

STMDeskly is a tool. What you put on it — and what happens because of it — is your responsibility. If someone contacts you through your Desk, that conversation is between you and them.

We're not responsible for losses caused by downtime, third-party services, or things outside our control.

8. Changes to these terms

If we change something important, we'll tell you. The date at the bottom of this page always shows the latest version.

Privacy — the short version

Here's everything we store about you, and why:

  • Email address — to log you in and send you important emails (like password resets).
  • Name, role, photos, services, location, WhatsApp, hours — everything you type into the editor. Only what you choose to share.
  • Your Desk link — the username you pick, so people can find you.

That's the whole list. We don't collect analytics beyond simple view counts, we don't fingerprint your device, we don't use cookies for anything except keeping you logged in.

Where your data lives

Your data is stored on Supabase — a database provider with servers in Europe. Passwords are encrypted. Photos are stored on the same servers.

If you're in Nigeria, your data is sent to Europe for storage. That's how most Nigerian platforms work. There's no local Nigerian hosting for services like this yet.

Who can see what

Public on your Desk: name, role, tagline, services, photos, location, hours, contact details — everything you filled in.

Private, only you: your email, your password, your account.

Not even we can see your password. Supabase hashes it — it can't be reversed.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from your editor. When you do, we remove your Desk, your photos, your account, and everything attached to it — permanently.

There's no "we'll keep it for 30 days just in case." It's gone.

Cookies

We use one cookie: your login session. That's it. No tracking cookies, no analytics cookies, no third-party cookies.
